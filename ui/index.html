Videos get re-encoded, GIFs get compressed, music becomes Opus with tags and cover art intact. Drop a whole album folder to do every track.

One download before you start

Converter uses ffmpeg for the encoding and opusenc for Opus audio. It can fetch both from their official builds (), check them against a fixed checksum, and keep them in its own folder.

Already have ffmpeg? Put ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe on your PATH and restart Converter.
